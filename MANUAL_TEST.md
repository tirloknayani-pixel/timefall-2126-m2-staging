Collect a cyan cell and amber material: both HUDs update once. Scan the beacon; go to WEST terminal and choose an approach. Different players power WEST and ground EAST within 45 seconds. Check one shared deadline, outcome and reserves.

Try risky bypass: red-zone drone pulses reduce Health and increase Alert. Let one explorer fall to zero, approach with the standing teammate and Revive; Health restores to 40. Leave the zone promptly. Disconnect/reload in the same tab within 90 seconds; identity, supplies, Health and event must persist. Host-reset and confirm all supplies/health/reserves/readiness reset. On another run choose a plan and withhold terminal contributions: server timeout applies 30 team damage and +15 Alert once.

Check portrait/landscape HUD, decision buttons, camera, joystick and performance on physical phones. M3 automated tests are software WebGL measurements; creator's M2 phone report does not count as M3 physical testing.
